Privacy Policy

App Name: Color Block Puzzle

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Color Block Puzzle ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile puzzle game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not require you to create an account, register, or submit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, home address, telephone number, or email address) to play Color Block Puzzle.

  • Local Game Data: Your gameplay progress, scores, unlocked themes, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device so you can continue playing where you left off.
  • No Personal Contacts or Media Access: The App does not access or collect your photos, videos, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Color Block Puzzle uses third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob to display advertisements (such as optional rewarded video ads that grant in-game bonuses when watched).

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal and device-level data for ad delivery, analytics, frequency capping, and fraud prevention, including:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • Coarse IP-based location (country/region level)
  • App interaction, ad performance, and basic diagnostic information

You can learn more about how Google collects and processes data by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms page: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Opt-Out & Advertising ID Controls: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Permissions Used

The App uses only standard system permissions necessary for core functionality:

  • Internet & Network State: Used to load advertisements and game audio resources over secure HTTPS connections.
  • Advertising ID: Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK for displaying advertisements and preventing fraud.
  • Vibration: Used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay (can be disabled anytime in the in-game Settings menu).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can permanently delete all saved game data at any time by:

  • Opening the in-game Settings menu and selecting Reset Progress, or
  • Clearing the App's storage data in your device's Android Settings, or
  • Uninstalling the App from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the email address below so that we can promptly delete such information.

7. Data Security

We value your trust and use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted HTTPS connections for network communication and private local storage for game progress.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
